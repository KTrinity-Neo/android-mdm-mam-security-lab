# Mobile Device Management (MDM)

## Objective

Understand Mobile Device Management and its role in enterprise cybersecurity.

## What is MDM?

Mobile Device Management (MDM) is a security and administration approach that allows organisations to centrally manage, monitor and secure mobile devices.

## Key Functions

- Device enrollment
- Security policy enforcement
- Password and screen-lock requirements
- Device compliance monitoring
- Remote management of enrolled devices
